## 🌟 Vision & Overview
Every student deserves world-class, barrier-free access to top-tier board exam preparation materials. **Class10 AI** removes all paywalls, subscription models, and complex login requirements, providing an all-in-one high-performance study platform designed specifically for the **CBSE / NCERT Class 10th Board Examinations**.

---

## 🚀 Key Modules & Capabilities

### 1. 📚 Chapter Notes Hub (1-Page & In-Depth)
- **1-Page Flash Recall Summaries**: High-yield cheat sheets featuring core board definitions, balanced chemical reactions, mathematical formulas, and topper presentation tips.
- **In-Depth Conceptual Notes**: Comprehensive topic guides covering Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), Mathematics, and Social Science (History, Geography, Civics, Economics).

### 2. 🎨 Visual Sketch Notes & Diagram Atlas
- Pure vector SVG concept sketch notes designed for rapid visual recall before entering the examination hall:
  - **Human Heart & Double Circulation Flowchart** (Valves, Chambers, Pulmonary loop, Septum).
  - **Concave Mirror Ray Diagrams** (Virtual, erect, and enlarged dentist case).
  - **Series vs. Parallel Electrical Circuits** (Current vs. Voltage division, domestic wiring rationale).
  - **Left-Hand Trigonometry Mnemonic** ($0^\circ, 30^\circ, 45^\circ, 60^\circ, 90^\circ$ finger rule).

### 3. 🏆 20-Year Solved PYQ Vault (2004 – 2024)
- A curated archive of real CBSE Delhi, All India, and Foreign board questions spanning two decades:
  - Categorized by **Year**, **Chapter**, and **Marks Weightage** (1M, 2M, 3M, 4M Case Study, 5M Theorems).
  - Step-by-step marking schemes breaking down partial marks per step.
  - Common pitfalls and topper presentation guidelines.
  - Offline bookmarking / saved question list.

### 4. 📝 Daily Practice Problems (DPP) & Instant Quizzes
- Topic-wise daily practice problem sets for all chapters.
- Interactive multiple-choice questions with timed feedback, automatic scoring, and detailed step-by-step explanations.
- Categorized by difficulty: **Basic**, **Standard**, and **HOTS (High Order Thinking Skills)**.

### 5. 📑 Sample Question Papers & Blueprints
- 80-mark, 3-hour model exam simulations following the latest CBSE blueprint.
- Time management strategies (15-minute cool-off reading plan, 35-minute Section A pacing).
- Direct print / save-as-PDF support for physical worksheet printing.

### 6. 🤖 Class 10 AI Guru (Interactive Doubt Solver)
- Embedded client-side AI assistant trained on NCERT concepts, formulas, and board exam strategies.
- Operates instantly with zero latency and zero cloud costs.
- Quick prompt chips for instantaneous formula derivations and study schedules.
